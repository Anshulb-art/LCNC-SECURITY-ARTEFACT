Artefact. The deliverable is a reproducible manual security testing protocol with a standardized evidence schema and a collector that indexes all proof into a manifest and zip. SAST is automated via run.ps (wrapped by scripts\sast\run-sast.ps1) and exports logs plus Sonar snapshots; DAST/UI are executed manually but follow a fixed folder/time-stamped structure so results are fully traceable. A one-command full orchestrator is documented as design/future work. The results may vary as the the platform gets updated, and developers also update their app.
